Questions, answered

Before you get started

Do I need experience before trying a project?

Not always. Start with tasks that match your current ability, read the requirements and be honest about what you can deliver.

What should I check before accepting a task?

Understand the expected output, deadline, eligibility requirements, review process and any payment terms shown on the official platform.

How can I stay safe online?

Use official channels, protect passwords and OTPs, involve a parent or trusted adult where appropriate, and never pay an unknown person to access a supposed opportunity.
