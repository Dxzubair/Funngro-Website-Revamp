A simple project workflow

From brief to review, without the guesswork.

A repeatable process helps teams set expectations and helps contributors focus on the work.

  • Write a clear objective and describe the target audience.
  • Specify the deliverable, format, deadline and review criteria.
  • Share approved resources and a safe communication channel.
  • Review the result against the brief and provide constructive feedback.

Questions, answered

Before publishing a project

What should a project brief include?

Objective, target audience, expected deliverable, scope, deadline, required resources, eligibility, review criteria and any relevant payment terms.

How should work be reviewed?

Use criteria that were shared in advance, provide specific feedback and avoid asking for work outside the agreed scope.
